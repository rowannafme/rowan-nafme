Rowan Music Education

Our Curriculum

Our Music Education program is a double major — a Bachelor of Music in Music Education and a Bachelor of Arts in Education — that prepares students to teach music at all levels, from kindergarten to grade 12. The program offers two tracks:

  • Instrumental Music Education
  • Vocal Music Education

For more information visit: Rowan University Music Education Webpage

Software & Prep

Software

  • Flat.io: Cloud-based music notation — create, edit, and share sheet music in your browser.
  • Musicca: Music theory and ear training games to sharpen fundamentals.
  • Soundtrap: Online collaborative DAW for recording, composing, and sharing music projects.
  • MuseScore: Free desktop notation software with MusicXML and PDF export.

Praxis Prep

  • Praxis Website: Official study materials, practice tests, and registration.
  • Mometrix Study Guide: Practice tests and test-taking strategies.
  • Kaplan Praxis Prep: Online lessons, practice tests, and coaching.
  • Quizlets:
    • Pedagogues in Music Education
    • Music Praxis 5113 Exam
