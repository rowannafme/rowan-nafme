Service

How NAfME serves our schools and campus community.

Rowan NAfME supports regional music education by volunteering at band, string, and vocal festivals, assisting local K–12 programs, and providing hands-on help for campus events.

We also provide opportunities for members to test lesson plans in a supportive environment and run peer tutoring and study groups for music majors who want extra support in theory, aural skills, conducting, music education coursework, and more.
